This agreement lets Kevin Pareek, who holds the copyright of Hoprig, use and relicense what you contribute, while
you keep your own copyright; it is not legal advice to you, so if you are unsure what it means for you, ask your
own lawyer before you agree.

It is built on the Apache Software Foundation's Individual Contributor License Agreement (version 2.2), with
Kevin Pareek in place of the Foundation and in plainer words. The numbered sections below follow that model.

## How you agree

Put this line in the description of your pull request, with your name:

> I have read the CLA and agree to it. — Your Name

The pull-request template has the line ready. The agreement covers your contributions to Hoprig, present and
future; the line in each pull request confirms it for what that pull request brings. A pull request without the
line is not merged.
