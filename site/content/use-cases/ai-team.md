# Coming: can I run an AI team under one assistant?

Not yet. This is planned: one assistant who runs several pieces of work at once, for development, research, content and posts, and brings everything that goes out to you for approval first.

## What exists today

**Tia** is an assistant in the dashboard: experimental, and off until you turn her on in **Settings → Tia**. She gets a coding job done for you: she opens a chat in a project, picks a provider among the ones you allow, sends the task, watches it and reports. Whatever costs money, changes your computer or opens it to the internet comes to you as a card first; the one exception is the level **Hands off**, where she may also use the paid API providers you allowed her without asking. Her own thinking runs on a provider you choose, so she costs money.

## What is planned

- Several jobs at once under one assistant, across development, research and content.
- Posts and other things that go out only after your approval.
